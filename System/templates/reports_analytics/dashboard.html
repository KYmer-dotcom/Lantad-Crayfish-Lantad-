► How is this computed?

1. Multi-year monthly totals are normalized into a baseline Seasonal Index ($\text{Index} = \frac{\text{Monthly Sales}}{\text{Average Month}}$).

2. Values > 1.10 trigger proactive pond preparation lead time ($\text{Lead Time} = \text{Harvest Date} - \text{Stocking Date}$).

3. Suggested stocking schedules account for an 8-week grow-out period to hit maximum harvest availability during holiday demand.
